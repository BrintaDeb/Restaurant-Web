## 📱 Mockup Descriptions

### 1. Dual Device Dining Showcase (`saffron-sage-social-showcase-1x1.jpg`)
- **Theme**: Luxury tabletop dining scene with Space Black MacBook Pro & flagship iPhone resting on dark charcoal marble and warm walnut.
- **Content**: Features the Saffron & Sage website with gold typography and Awadhi cuisine alongside the mobile ordering app with live cart and ₹ checkout.
- **Vibe**: Saffron threads, brass bowls, warm candlelights, and royal dining ambiance.

### 2. Royal Hall Cinematic Banner (`saffron-sage-social-banner-16x9.jpg`)
- **Theme**: Wide panoramic studio presentation inside the royal dining hall with golden arches and warm amber glow.
- **Content**: Centered MacBook Pro highlighting "The Royal Experience" & story sections, flanked by the smartphone displaying royal curries & tandoor selections.
- **Vibe**: Ultra-high-end luxury fine dining atmosphere, perfect for wide social headers and LinkedIn announcements.

### 3. Vertical Mobile Story Spotlight (`saffron-sage-social-story-9x16.jpg`)
- **Theme**: Immersive 9:16 mobile-first story graphic.
- **Content**: Large floating iPhone in titanium showing Awadhi Dum Biryani and Murgh Makhani dishes with gold "Order Online" floating checkout CTA, backed by the laptop site view and glowing votives.
- **Vibe**: High-converting engagement creative for Instagram Stories, TikTok, and Reels.

### 4. 3D Isometric Luxury Podium (`saffron-sage-3d-isometric-1x1.jpg`)
- **Theme**: Minimalist 3D architectural podium with gold rim lighting and floating saffron dust.
- **Content**: Angled floating iPhone displaying the interactive delivery cart alongside the MacBook Pro showing the Table Reservation system.
- **Vibe**: Tech-forward, high-fashion culinary design presentation.
